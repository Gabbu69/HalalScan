# HalalScan design
Refine the incumbent green, cream, and gold identity for an everyday shopping tool.

- Operate mode: visible scan entry, simple barcode/photo/text choices, evidence-first results.
- Amiri for the wordmark and home heading; Nunito for readable task interfaces. Locally bundled fonts.
- Warm cream canvas, restrained green actions, gold detail, light paper surfaces; matching dark tokens.
- Desktop: horizontal navigation and two-column home/results. Mobile: four-item bottom navigation and a single reading column.
- Real scan records only. No decorative stats, retailer claims, or model confidence badges.
- Accessible focus, 44px minimum touch controls, reduced motion, and RTL layouts.
