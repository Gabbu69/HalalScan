# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Everyday shoppers checking packaged-food ingredients on a phone, including in shops with weak connectivity.

## Product Purpose
Explain ingredient screening evidence and unresolved sources so shoppers can make informed choices. HalalScan does not issue or verify product certificates.

## Capabilities and Constraints
React/Vite frontend, Flask local API, Vercel TypeScript adapter, maintained JSON rules. Device-local saved scans and favorites without accounts. Offline saved checks and guidance. English ingredient matching; English, Tagalog, and Arabic UI. Three product results: no flagged ingredients, non-compliant, needs verification.

## Brand Commitments
Keep the HalalScan name, existing logo, and green, cream, and gold identity.

## Evidence on Hand
Existing ingredient-rule references and historical academic artifacts. No verified retailer catalog or live product-certificate verification. Historical evaluations do not validate the shopper policy.

## Product Principles
Scanning is the primary task. Missing evidence remains unresolved. A recognized certifier name is not product certification. Saved data belongs to the device. Explain problems and the next action plainly.
